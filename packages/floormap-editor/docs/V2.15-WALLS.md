# Walls, split locks and overlap blocking (Phase 2)

## Split lock
The single lock is now two independent flags per room (`layout.positionLocked`, `layout.sizeLocked`), set with the **Lock position** and **Lock size** checkboxes in the room panel.

| | position locked | size locked |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | the room's top-left corner cannot move | width and height cannot change |
| Drag the room | refused | allowed |
| Resize handles | only bottom-right (top-left would move the corner) | none; the Width/Height fields are disabled |
| Wall drag | its left/top wall cannot be dragged; it cannot be pushed | it can only be carried along as a whole |
| Run mapper | never moved | moved, size kept |

Old files keep working: `layout.locked: true` is read as both flags, and explicit flags win when present. When both flags are set the editor also writes `locked: true`, so older readers still see a locked room. Rooms are tinted: green = both, yellow = position only, violet = size only.

## Block overlaps
A **Block overlaps** checkbox (default on) applies to the Slide modes and the wall tool. A move that would make two rooms overlap that did not overlap before stops at the last position before contact. The drag is swept from its start in grid steps, so a room cannot jump through another room even if the pointer ends on the far side. Rooms that already overlap are not frozen. **Free** mode is unchanged and may still overlap.

## Drag walls tool
**Tool > Drag walls (resize to fit)** shows a grab strip on every wall of every room (hover highlights it). Dragging a wall along its normal moves it:
- the room on the other side of an attached wall resizes with it (every neighbour on that wall follows, e.g. Kitchen and Front Hall on the Dining wall);
- a room that reaches its minimum size (50% of its category default, at least 1 m) is pushed along and its own neighbours resize in turn ("squeeze, then push");
- rooms that are not attached are never pushed: they stop the wall (overlap blocking), unless **Resize touching rooms too** is on (default): a room that merely touches the wall is then resized and pushed like an attached one (see "Touching rooms" below). The hidden `Outside` room never blocks.

Hard stops, with a message in the status line:
- a position-locked room that would have to move, or whose left/top wall is dragged;
- a room reaching its maximum size (250% of its category default; a room that is already larger keeps its size as the limit);
- a door or passage that would be cut off, or a wall running along the drag that would lose its shared part;
- an unattached room in the way (when "Resize touching rooms too" is off, or the room does not touch the wall).

Connector points keep their world position, and the opening of an attached pair stays where it was, so connectors stay green. The result is exact: the wall moves as far as it can, not a scaled-back fraction.

Code: `src/walls.ts` (`planWall`, pure, embedded into the HTML) and the hooks `wallLayer`, `applyWall`, `blockMsg` in `src/editor.ts`. Model: per axis, every room has two variables (its two walls); all rules are difference constraints `v_j - v_i >= c`, repaired by pushing in the drag direction (negative drags are solved on mirrored coordinates).

## Corner handles (Slide modes)
In **Slide** and **Slide + pull** mode the corner resize handles use the wall solver: a corner drag is two wall drags (x first, then y on the result), so neighbours are squeezed and pushed, locks, maximum sizes, doors and **Block overlaps** are honoured, and connectors stay green. Each axis stops on its own at the last feasible step; the status line names the first blocker. Sizes are limited like the wall tool (minimum 50% of the category default, at least 1 m; maximum 250%) instead of the flat 1 m minimum of Free mode. **Free** mode keeps the original corner resize (one room only, no pushing). Code: `planCorner` in `src/walls.ts` (pure, embedded after `planWall`), hooks `applyCorner` / `applyWallResult` in `src/editor.ts`; tests in `test/corners.test.ts`.

## Touching rooms (`glueTouching`)
Rooms that merely touch, with no relationship (no door) between them, used to be obstacles: dragging a wall against one stopped with "would overlap". The checkbox **Resize touching rooms too** (default on) treats such a pair like an attached wall without an opening: the neighbour is resized, squeezed to its minimum and then pushed. Locks, maximum sizes and unrelated rooms further away still stop the wall. Pairs that have a relationship (attached, broken or detached on purpose) are not affected by this rule. Used by the wall tool and by Slide-mode corner handles; off = the previous behaviour.

## Locks
The **Lock position** / **Lock size** checkboxes apply immediately (one undo step each); Apply is only needed for Width/Height.

## Wall alignments
- **Add alignment** snaps the chosen wall of every selected room to the level of the **first selected room (the reference)**, using the wall solver (one wall drag per room; the reference and walls already aligned are held). A wall that cannot get there (locks, sizes, doors, overlaps) moves as far as it can; the status line names it and the reason. One undo step. The reference is shown as a thick dark-blue wall when a member is selected, and as "(reference)" in the alignment list.
- **Wall tool and Slide-mode corners** keep walls of a group that are at the same level now at the same level (dragging one wall carries the others, with resizing, pushing, locks and overlap blocking as usual). Walls of a group that are not at the same level are left alone until they are aligned.
- A group whose walls are no longer level (for example after a Free resize) is listed as **Inactive** (which room is how far off the reference) with a **Re-align** button, which runs the same snap as "Add alignment" (one undo step). Free mode is free: it never removes or updates a group. The list shows **Active** when all walls are level again.
- A drag distance that is not a multiple of the grid step now ends exactly on the target (last step shorter).
- **Move rooms** in Slide modes still ignores alignment groups; Free mode moves the whole group as before.

## Not covered yet

- Corner handles in Free mode still resize one room only (by design: Free is unchanged).
- Rectangular rooms only: polygons need the wall-graph model from the plan.
