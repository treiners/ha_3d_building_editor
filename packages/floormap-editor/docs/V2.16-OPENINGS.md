# Add connector: more doors, exterior doors and windows

Connectors tab, first panel *Add connector* (`+ Door`, `+ Passage`, `+ Window`; the help text is a hover tip on the title). Until now a connector could only be created by **Connect** (one door per room pair, always at the middle of the shared wall) or by *Add room*; windows only existed when the loaded file already had them. This closes that gap. Floor-plan mode only (not in the Gap view or in entity mode).

## Using it
1. Optionally set the default *width* and *door type* in Settings (gear, section Connectors); they are used here too.
2. Click `+ Door`, `+ Passage` or `+ Window`, then move over a room: a green bar shows where the opening would land and how wide it is.
3. Click on a wall to place it. The new connector is selected (its card opens). One undo step. The type is armed once; **Shift** keeps it armed, **Alt** places it freely (no grid snap), **Esc** or the same button cancels.

## Rules (`addOpening` in `src/edit.ts`, pure)
- The wall is the nearest wall of any room within 0.5 m of the click; the position is the click along that wall (grid snapped).
- **Door / passage on a wall shared with another room** (facing walls touch within 0.08 m, shared part at least width + 0.1 m, click inside it): a connected pair, both connectors at the same point plus the relationship. This is how a **second door between the same two rooms** works. The opening is kept inside the shared part.
- **Anywhere else on a wall** (no neighbour, or the part of the wall that is not shared): an unpaired **exterior** opening. It is drawn orange (not connected) and has no relationship. The hidden `Outside` room can still be the partner of a door when its rectangle touches the wall (as in the reference home), but it is never the wall that gets hit.
- **Windows are always unpaired**, also on a shared wall. Window sill and head heights are edited in the connector card as before.
- The opening keeps half its width (at least 0.05 m) from the wall ends, and 0.1 m from the next opening on that wall (both walls for a pair); otherwise it is refused with the reason (*Too close to another opening*, *wall too short*, *Click on a wall of a room*).
- Ids: `<room>-<side>-<type>`, then `-2`, `-3` ...; a pair's relationship is `<idA>--<idB>`. Width (and door type, doors only) are stored like *Connect* does.
- *Connect two selected rooms* is unchanged (first connection, middle of the shared wall). Its refusal for an already connected pair now points to *Add connector*; the button now lives in the Add connector panel.
